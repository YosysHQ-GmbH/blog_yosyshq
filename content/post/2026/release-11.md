The November release of Tabby CAD Suite is now available for download. This version includes under-the-hood improvements to performance and bugfixes. Some notable changes affecting customers that maintain their own custom plugins or compile on their own:

* Strings have been reimplemented with prefix deduplication to improve performance. Please refer to docs/source/yosys_internals/idstring.rst on how to convert your plugins/custom passes.

In other YosysHQ news:
